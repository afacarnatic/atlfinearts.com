Two-Day Program

Workshop Curriculum

Each day explores a distinct tradition within the Carnatic canon — from the devotional heritage of padams to the rarest compositions in the repertoire. $120 per person.

✦ All proceeds go directly to the artist. Atlanta Fine Arts is only facilitating this workshop.

1

Day One

Tuesday, April 21, 2026

Compositions Covered

  • Dasar Padam
  • Swati Tirunal
  • Annamayya

Exploring the devotional and lyrical traditions of the saint-composers — padams, keertanas, and the poetic depth they carry.

2

Day Two

Wednesday, April 22, 2026

Compositions Covered

  • Rare compositions of the Trinity

— or —

  • Rare Tamil compositions

A rare opportunity to learn compositions seldom taught in the classroom — drawn from the vast, underexplored body of work by Thyagaraja, Dikshitar & Syama Sastri, or from the Tamil Carnatic tradition.
